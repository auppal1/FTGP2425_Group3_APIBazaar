Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about publishing and consuming on the platform.

Do I need to rewrite my existing API?

No. Just give us your endpoint, sit back, relax, and let the earnings roll in. Our API gateway verifies a caller's tokens by scanning the immutable blockchain.

How do I know my endpoint won't be abused?

Tokens are dished out on a daily basis and are capped at your capacity. Our bonding curves are configured so prices exponentially increase near capacity. If someone wants to attack, they make you a millionaire in the process. We know which we would choose...

How does API Bazaar benefit buyers?

With far lower comissions comes far lower prices. We HATE subscriptions and LOVE transparent, pay-as-you-go models. Smart contracts are designed such that prices are immutable during the current day, meaning no suprises!
